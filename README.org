* Core Philosophy

BanglaChan (বাংলাচান) is for Bengali netizens to express their
thoughts without revealing their identity on the internet. Most forums
and social networks require multitude of identifications for users
including name, self-portrait, pronouns, etc. With the loss in
anonymity, not only the discussants are forced to give out their
personal information, discussants can never have an open-minded
conversation there. Discussants are judged by their name, how they
look, and what socioeconomic class and subculture they belong to. A
comment on such networks are always about who is saying it rather than
what they are saying. BanglaChan strips away all forms of
identification of an user leaving only what they say. The whole
conversation is represented in a form of a socratic dialogue. The site
features several boards for various interests. Users can post new
threads, reply to an existing thread, hotlink images, reference other
posts, and quote other users without having to register an account or
pay a poisha or personal data. Each thread or reply on BanglaChan has
an unique ID and a hashed user ID indicating the person who posted it.
The user ID is unique per thread to preserve users' anonymity across
threads.
